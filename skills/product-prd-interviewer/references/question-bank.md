# Product Discovery Question Bank

Use this only when the live interview needs more depth. Do not dump the full list into the conversation. Select the few questions that best reduce uncertainty.

## Product Framing

- What problem should this product solve, in one sentence?
- Who feels this problem most often or most painfully?
- What happens today when the problem is not solved well?
- Why is now the right time to build this?
- What would make this product clearly successful 3 to 6 months after launch?

## Users and Personas

- Who is the primary user?
- Are there admins, managers, buyers, operators, or reviewers besides the end user?
- What level of technical comfort should the product assume?
- Where will the user be when using it: work desk, mobile, field, meeting, home?
- What motivates the user to come back?

## Workflow

- What is the first action a user should take after opening the app?
- What is the most important repeated workflow?
- What information does the user need before making a decision?
- What should happen when the happy path fails?
- What manual work should the product eliminate or reduce?

## Scope

- What are the top 3 must-have capabilities for the first usable version?
- What should explicitly not be included in v1?
- Which features are emotionally exciting but not necessary for launch?
- What is the smallest version that would still prove value?
- What decisions can wait until after user feedback?

## Data, Integrations, and Permissions

- What data does the app create, read, update, or delete?
- Where does the data come from today?
- Does the product need login, roles, or approval flows?
- Are there external systems to integrate with?
- What data needs to be exported, reported, or audited?

## Business and Adoption

- Is this for internal use, customers, or a public market?
- Who decides whether it gets adopted?
- What behavior needs to change for adoption to happen?
- What pricing, cost saving, or ROI logic matters?
- What support or onboarding will users need?

## Quality and Constraints

- Does it need to work on mobile, desktop, or both?
- Are accessibility, localization, or offline use important?
- What performance expectations matter most?
- Are there legal, privacy, compliance, or security constraints?
- Is there a launch deadline or budget constraint?

## Metrics

- What user action best signals activation?
- What should increase, decrease, or become faster?
- What retention behavior matters?
- What operational metric would prove the app is saving time or money?
- What analytics events should be tracked from day one?

## Risks and Assumptions

- What assumption would break the product if it were false?
- What is the riskiest part: user adoption, data availability, UX, technical feasibility, or business model?
- What dependencies are outside the team's control?
- What should be prototyped or validated before building the full app?
- What open question should be answered first?
