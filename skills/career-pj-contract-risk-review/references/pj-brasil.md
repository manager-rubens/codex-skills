# PJ Brazil Reference

Use this reference when reviewing a Brazilian individual PJ service arrangement. It is derived from the Zignet project context file `docs/contexto-pj-brasil.md` created on 2026-05-20.

This is informational context, not legal advice.

## Core Distinction

A PJ arrangement is safer when it is a real commercial relationship:

- independent parties;
- written service contract;
- defined scope, deliverables, price, deadlines, and acceptance criteria;
- payment by invoice/nota fiscal;
- autonomy over method, schedule, and organization;
- ability to serve other clients;
- records of deliveries, approvals, invoices, and scope changes.

The main risk is irregular pejotizacao: using a CNPJ to mask an employment relationship.

## Employment-Like Risk Elements

Watch for the classic CLT risk factors:

- pessoalidade: the company requires that exact person, with no real substitute/support possibility;
- non-eventual/habitual work: continuous work inserted into the company's permanent routine;
- onerosidade: regular payment for labor;
- subordinacao: hierarchical command, direct orders, discipline, control of how work is done, or workday supervision.

Remote work does not remove risk. Digital command, control, and supervision can still indicate legal subordination.

## High-Risk Signals

- fixed mandatory schedule, time tracking, login monitoring, or daily availability like an employee;
- internal manager giving hierarchical orders about how to work, not only what to deliver;
- employee-like benefits, internal HR policies, vacation approval, warning/discipline process;
- company email, job title, org chart, badge, or public presentation as an employee;
- monthly payment without clear scope, invoice, deliverables, or evidence of acceptance;
- practical exclusivity due to workload or availability requirements;
- ex-CLT converted to PJ to perform the same role with same boss and routine;
- PJ doing permanent team work indistinguishable from employees;
- obligation to use company equipment/processes beyond security/access needs;
- broad non-compete, liability, penalty, or termination clauses that transfer excessive risk to the contractor.

## Contract Points To Review

The contract should usually address:

- parties and CNPJ details;
- object/scope of services;
- deliverables, milestones, acceptance criteria, and deadlines;
- payment, invoice flow, tax responsibility, reimbursement, and late payment;
- autonomy of method, schedule, and organization;
- absence of employment relationship, with practices aligned to the wording;
- ability to serve other clients and limits on exclusivity;
- confidentiality, data protection/LGPD, security access, and document return/deletion;
- intellectual property assignment/licensing and portfolio/reference rights;
- change-order process for new scope;
- termination notice, cure periods, fees due, handoff, and survival clauses;
- reasonable limitation of liability and proportional penalties;
- forum/dispute method and governing law;
- compliance, anti-corruption, and conflict-of-interest rules if relevant.

Avoid employee-like wording such as:

- cargo, chefe, subordinado, jornada, ponto, escala, banco de horas;
- ferias autorizadas by manager;
- advertencia/discipline;
- salario, folha, beneficios trabalhistas;
- obrigacao de cumprir ordens internas as if employee.

Prefer commercial wording such as:

- prestador, contratada, consultor, fornecedor;
- escopo, entregaveis, milestones, SLA, aceite;
- disponibilidade comercial, janela de atendimento, prazo de resposta;
- comunicacao de indisponibilidade and continuity plan;
- criterio tecnico, qualidade, revisao, correcao, suporte.

## Legal Anchors To Remember

CLT art. 2 defines employer around economic risk, admission, payment, and direction of personal services.

CLT art. 3 defines employee around personal service, non-eventual work, dependence/subordination, and salary.

CLT art. 6 says remote/distance work can still be employment if employment elements exist; digital command/control can be equivalent to in-person supervision.

CLT art. 9 makes acts intended to distort, prevent, or defraud CLT protections null.

CLT art. 442-B recognizes autonomous work when legal formalities are met, but the practical facts must still support autonomy.

The Brazilian Civil Code governs service provision when the relationship is not subject to labor law or special law.

Law 6.019/1974, as amended, regulates third-party service provision and supports services even in core activities when requirements are met.

STF jurisprudence has broadly accepted outsourcing and other forms of work division between legal entities. As of the 2025 decision in Theme 1389 / ARE 1.532.603, national proceedings about pejotizacao questions were suspended until final judgment on competence, legality, and burden of proof. Verify current status before relying on this point.

## Contractor Duties And Planning

A PJ contractor should plan for:

- active CNPJ and compatible CNAE/activity;
- correct invoices;
- taxes and social security/accounting obligations;
- accountant support;
- reserve for taxes, vacation, illness, downtime, equipment, software, insurance, and private benefits;
- documentation of contracts, invoices, payment, scope, approvals, and communications;
- confidentiality, LGPD, IP, security, and professional liability obligations.

## Career And Negotiation Considerations

Do not compare PJ monthly amount with CLT net salary only. Account for:

- taxes and accountant;
- unpaid vacation and downtime;
- health plan, insurance, pension, and sick leave;
- 13th salary equivalent, FGTS equivalent, and termination risk;
- equipment, software, training, and office costs;
- currency/payment delay risk;
- commercial margin for uncertainty.

If the real need is fixed availability, direct hierarchy, permanent employee-like team integration, and little autonomy, flag that CLT/EOR or another employment model may better match the reality.
