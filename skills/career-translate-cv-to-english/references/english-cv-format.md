# English CV Format

Use this reference when the user supplies or requests the recruiter-style English CV format. It captures structure and formatting behavior only; do not copy sample CV content.

## Layout

Preferred order:

1. Candidate name.
2. Contact line with `Phone`, `E-mail`, location, and LinkedIn.
3. `Professional Summary`.
4. `Core Competencies`.
5. `Professional Experience`.
6. `Education`.
7. `Certifications & Development`.
8. Optional `Additional Development & Experience` to preserve older experience or extra training without adding a `Languages` section.

## Writing Pattern

- Summary: one compact paragraph tailored to the target job.
- Competencies: grouped labeled lines, usually `Leadership`, `Technical Expertise`, `AI & Innovation`, and `Strategy & Operations`.
- Experience: short label plus action/scope/outcome, for example `People Management: Led performance reviews...`.
- Dates: English month abbreviations, such as `Sep 2022 - Dec 2023`.
- Tone: concise, ATS-friendly, natural English. Avoid literal translation and inflated recruiter cliches.

## Safeguards

- Treat any sample English CV as a formatting reference only.
- Do not copy sample claims, language proficiency, achievements, or course selection.
- Do not add a `Languages` section unless explicitly requested.
- If adapting Ruben's CV, preserve the required AI tools and Anthropic course entries when present.
